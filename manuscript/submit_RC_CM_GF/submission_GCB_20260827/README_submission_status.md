## Borrador de respuestas del formulario (máx. 50 palabras cada una; revisar)

- **Scientific question:** Are the ranges of Iberian steppe birds demographically sustainable, or are they transient legacies maintained by past landscapes? We ask whether colonisation can offset local extinction once imperfect detection is accounted for.
- **Key findings:** Detection-corrected colonisation is two to six orders of magnitude lower than extinction in all four species. Equilibrium occupancy falls far below observed ranges, indicating a large extinction debt and a demographic trap.
- **Importance and timeliness:** Pattern-based monitoring cannot detect colonisation collapse. We show range contraction can outpace habitat loss even under moderate environmental change, redirecting conservation towards preventing local extinction rather than promoting recolonisation.
- **Fit to GCB:** Biological: metapopulation dynamics of four threatened steppe birds. Global change: agricultural intensification, land-use and climate drivers of colonisation and extinction, and the use of citizen-science data for global change inference.
- **Three most recent relevant papers:** Herrando et al. 2026 (*Conservation Biology*); Chamizo et al. 2025 (*Biological Conservation*); Gómez-Catasús et al. 2025 (*Biological Conservation*).
